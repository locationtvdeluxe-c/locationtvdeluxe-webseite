ABC Islands Cruise: Guide and Tips

We're Nadine and Sebastian from LocationTV Deluxe. Our e-book helps you choose what is actually worth your time at each stop on a cruise from La Romana – on land and in the water.

The e-book is in German; this page is the English overview of the same route.

🇩🇪 Deutsche Version: ABC Inseln Kreuzfahrt

What's in the guide

  • What you can do on foot in the port towns – and when you need a boat or a transfer
  • The strongest snorkeling and dive sites on the route, described honestly
  • Details that change the day: the rules on Bonaire, the right season in Samaná
  • When you should plan to be back at the ship with time to spare
  • Short tips for filming your port days

The ports at a glance

La Romana

Start and finish – and the way to an artists' village, a cave, and the dive sites off Bayahíbe.

Isla Catalina

An uninhabited protected island: the strongest water day off the Dominican coast.

Santo Domingo

A UNESCO colonial city – if you come ashore at the right terminal.

Samaná

A nature stop that only fully opens in a certain season.

Tortola

Road Town on foot; the islands' best-known wreck only by boat.

Antigua

Nelson's Dockyard is worth it, but it isn't next to the ship.

Aruba

A walk from the pier, snorkeling on the northwest coast, and a famous wreck out on the water.

Curaçao

Colorful Willemstad on foot – and shallow shore reefs close by.

Bonaire

The clearest reef on the route, with rules you should know before you go.

A few questions about the guide

Who is the guide for?

For everyone on the trip: whether you prefer to stay in town, lie on the beach, or snorkel and dive. For each stop, the guide sets out what fits – and when you are better off booking an excursion.

Which ports does the e-book cover?

A round trip from and back to La Romana: Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao, plus Isla Catalina, Santo Domingo, Samaná, Tortola and Antigua. For each stop, the guide tells you what is worth doing on land and in the water.
