What to Expect at the Rüdesheim Christmas Market

Every winter, the historic old town of Rüdesheim am Rhein and the world-famous Drosselgasse transform into a festive wonderland. More than 20 nations from four continents bring their unique traditions, crafts, and culinary specialties to over 100 decorated stalls.

Key Details for 2026:

Dates: 19 November – 23 December 2026, open daily.

Location: Historic Old Town & Drosselgasse, Rüdesheim am Rhein
