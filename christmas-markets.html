German Christmas Markets 2026: all dates at a glance

Here are all the Christmas markets from our guide with their 2026 dates. In the guide you'll find the opening times for each market, our highlights and our tips on what's really worth it.

Rüdesheim am Rhein: Christmas Market of Nations

19 November – 23 December 2026, open daily. More than 20 nations from four continents present their goods, specialities and Christmas traditions at over 100 stalls, winding through the historic old town right into the famous Drosselgasse.

Wiesbaden: Sternschnuppenmarkt (Shooting Star Market)

24 November – 23 December 2026. From the main station it's about a 15-minute walk along Bahnhofstraße to the market, which begins in front of the historic town hall, opposite the Stadtschloss (City Palace).

Frankfurt am Main: Frankfurt Christmas Market at the Römer

23 November – 22 December 2026. Frankfurt's big Christmas market is a real magnet for international visitors, and its heart beats at the Römer. Above the rooftops, the City Christmas Market adds a view of the skyline.

Bensheim: Bensheim Christmas Market

26 November – 22 December 2026. Half-timbered houses, fairy lights and the big Christmas tree on the market square. Not a giant market, and that's exactly what makes it so pleasant.

Worms: Wormser Weihnacht (Worms Christmas)

20 November – 23 December 2026. In the City of the Nibelungs, Christmas leads you from Lutherplatz to the Obermarkt beneath the Kaiserdom. Our tip: the Turmblasen (tower brass music) on the fourth Sunday of Advent, 20 December 2026.

Alzey: Alzeyer Weihnachtstreff (Alzey Christmas Get-Together)

23 November – 23 December 2026. Small traders around the big square, then a walk through a huge archway right into the town centre.

Mannheim: three Christmas markets in one day

23 November – 23 December 2026. The Märchenwald (Fairy-Tale Forest) on Paradeplatz, the Kapuzinerplanken and the Wasserturm (Water Tower). Sebastian's recommendation for photographers: the Kapuzinerplanken.

Speyer: Christmas and New Year Market

23 November 2026 – 10 January 2027. Once you've walked through the Altpörtel (the old town gate), the colourful bustle begins against the backdrop of the Kaiserdom (Imperial Cathedral).

Deidesheim: Deidesheimer Advent

On the four Advent weekends, 27 November – 20 December 2026. Advent in the Palatinate hardly gets more romantic: around 80 stalls stretch from the railway station to the historic market square.

Bad Wimpfen: Old German Christmas Market

First three Advent weekends 2026 only. Half-timbered houses, fairy lights and the silhouette of the old Kaiserpfalz (imperial palace). Important: bring your own mug.

Extra: Nuremberg Christkindlesmarkt

27 November – 24 December 2026. Nuremberg lies well outside our region, but we didn't want to leave it out, especially if you're travelling from abroad.
